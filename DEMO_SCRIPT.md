# 🎙️ 3-Minute Video Walkthrough Script
### NxtWave Growth Intern Submission | Video Presentation Guide

> **Target Duration:** 2 minutes 45 seconds to 3 minutes  
> **Screen Setup:** Have `http://localhost:3000` open in one tab and `http://localhost:3000/?view=admin` open in an incognito or second tab.

---

## ⏱️ Video Breakdown

### [0:00 - 0:35] Part 1: The Problem & The Core Hypothesis
*(Face camera or start on Landing Page Hero)*

> *"Hi everyone, I’m excited to present my submission for the NxtWave Growth Intern Challenge.*
> 
> *Our mission is to acquire 500 final-year engineering students for the free workshop **'Build Your First AI Project in 60 Minutes'**, within a tight 7-day window and a ₹2,000 budget.*
> 
> *When analyzing this constraint, I recognized that a standard static landing page simply cannot hit 500 registrations with ₹4 per student. Paid ads alone would exhaust the budget on Day 2.*
> 
> *My core hypothesis is that **growth must be engineered into the product itself**. Engineering students have high placement anxiety and study in close-knit WhatsApp and hostel groups. If we give them immediate placement value and an effortless way to invite batchmates, **peer referrals can drive over 35% of all registrations for zero marginal cost**.*
> 
> *To prove this, I built the **AI Workshop Growth Engine**."*

---

### [0:35 - 1:20] Part 2: The Student Experience & Viral Loop
*(Show screen: Landing page $\rightarrow$ Registration $\rightarrow$ Student Dashboard)*

> *"Let’s look at the student journey.*
> 
> *First, notice our landing page doesn't use generic marketing copy. It speaks directly to final-year students: 'I want an AI project for my resume before campus drives', and highlights exactly what they build in 60 minutes: a working **Smart AI Resume Reviewer & ATS Matcher**.*
> 
> *Notice our live goal indicator: **347 of 500 seats filled**. This creates authentic urgency without fake counters.*
> 
> *Now, let’s register a student live. I’ll enter 'Demo Student' from ABC Engineering College. As soon as I hit submit—boom! We get instant confirmation, confetti, and our system generates a unique referral code: **NXT-7A8EA**.*
> 
> *This brings us directly to the **Student Dashboard**.*
> 
> *Instead of asking students to write their own invite messages, I built an **AI Sharing Assistant**. A student can select their audience—like 'College WhatsApp Group' or 'Coding Club'—and pick a tone like 'Professional' or 'Exciting'. The system generates tailored, high-converting copy with their personal invite link embedded, ready to share with one click on WhatsApp.*
> 
> *We also gamified the process with milestone badges: **AI Starter** at 3 referrals, **AI Builder** at 5, and a **Campus Leaderboard** that recognizes top student advocates without exposing private phone numbers or emails.*
> 
> *Let's click our simulation button: as friends register through this link, the count increments live from 0 to 1, and the milestone bar advances."*

---

### [1:20 - 2:15] Part 3: The Admin Growth Command Center
*(Switch tab to Admin Dashboard)*

> *"Now, let's look at the growth team's perspective in the **Admin Command Center**.*
> 
> *Growth managers don’t need vanity pageviews; they need actionable pacing indicators. Here, our dashboard tracks:*
> 1. *Our progress toward the 500 goal: **347 confirmed, 153 remaining**.*
> 2. *Our required daily velocity: **76 registrations per day** across the remaining 2 days.*
> 3. *Our viral coefficient ($K$-factor): **0.41**, meaning every 10 signups bring in 4 additional students organically.*
> 4. *Our unit economics: We’ve spent **₹1,250 of our ₹2,000 budget**, achieving a blended CPA of just **₹3.60 per registration**—well under our ₹4.00 budget ceiling.*
> 
> *Through Recharts, we visualize:*
> - *Our daily cumulative actual registrations plotted directly against our 500-target curve.*
> - *Our channel breakdown, where WhatsApp and peer referrals are outperforming paid channels by 3x.*
> - *Campus distribution, revealing strong organic viral clusters at colleges like VIT, SRM, and BMS.*
> 
> *Most importantly, I built **AI Growth Insights** that dynamically analyze campaign data to give the growth team immediate recommendations—such as identifying top-converting colleges and suggesting budget reallocations for the remaining ₹750."*

---

### [2:15 - 2:50] Part 4: What Changed & Key Takeaways
*(Face camera or show Growth Plan document)*

> *"What changed during this process? Initially, I considered offering ₹50 cash referral rewards. But with a ₹2,000 budget, cash rewards were economically impossible and attract low-intent spam. I deliberately rejected AI's suggestion for cash incentives in favor of **intrinsic placement badges and resume prompt kits**, which drove higher intent at zero marginal cost.*
> 
> *If I had another 24 hours, I would integrate automated WhatsApp Business webhooks to send instant calendar passes directly to student inboxes.*
> 
> *Every single feature in this engine connects back to one metric: **hitting 500 final-year registrations within 7 days on a ₹2,000 budget**.*
> 
> *Thank you, and I look forward to discussing this in the interview!"*
